*Release Notes*:

In this CANVAS release we are bringing you 4 new modules and bugfixes.

Our new modules include zerologon (CVE-2020-1742) and three remote code execution
exploits targeting SaltStack (CVE-2020-11651), Apache Solr (CVE-2020-13957) and
Solaris 10 (CVE-2020-14817).
